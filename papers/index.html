ElectAnon: A Blockchain-Based, Anonymous, Robust and Scalable Ranked-Choice Voting Protocol

Ceyhun Onur, Arda Yurdakul

Distributed Ledger Technologies: Research and Practice · 2023
arXiv:2204.00057 · 2022 preprint

[ ACM ] ↗[ arXiv ] ↗[ arXiv PDF ] ↗[ Code ] ↗

ElectAnon is a ranked-choice voting protocol built on a blockchain. It uses zero-knowledge proofs to let voters cast ballots anonymously and timed state machines to reduce authorities' direct control over the voting process. The work evaluates scalability through gas costs and supports interchangeable tallying libraries.
